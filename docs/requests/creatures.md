# Requests / hand-off notes from `creatures`

## world-life (NPC AI) — how to use the creature models
All models: `res://assets/models/npc/<id>.glb`, listed in `res://assets/models/npc/catalog.json`
(id, file, kind, npc_id, role, clips, looping, walk/run speeds, triangle count).

* Skeleton path is always `Armature/Skeleton3D`; one `AnimationPlayer` per model. Clips are **in place**
  (no root motion); move the NPC node at the speed listed in the catalog (`walk_speed`, `speeds`) so feet do
  not slide. Looping clips have `loop_mode = LINEAR` set in the `.glb.import` files
  (`blender/npc/configure_import.py`), one-shots: `takeoff`, `land`, `bark`, `caw`, `hop`.
* Front is +Z (`Basis.looking_at(dir, Vector3.UP, true)`), origin on the ground between the feet.
* **Humans** (`ba_tu co_hoa chu_sau bin ped_office ped_student ped_elder_man ped_helmet_woman ped_shopper
  ped_xeom`) share one bone layout (`root pelvis spine_01 spine_02 chest neck head jaw clavicle_* upperarm_*
  forearm_* hand_* fingers_* thumb_* thigh_* shin_* foot_* toe_*`), so any clip can be retargeted between them.
  Clips: `idle walk sit talk sell pet_cat shoo look_around carry phone` (all loops).
  - `sit` is authored for a **0.25 m plastic stool**: put the NPC origin where the stool stands (the
    pelvis rests 0.25 m + ~9 cm above the ground, feet 0.3 m in front).
  - `pet_cat`: crouched, right hand strokes ~0.3 m in front of the toes at ~0.25 m height — place the cat
    there (facing either way).
  - `carry` is a walking cycle with both hands holding a load at the waist (same speed as `walk`).
  - Props in hands: add a `BoneAttachment3D` on `hand_R` / `hand_L`. Accessories that are part of the
    meshes: cô Hoa's nón lá, chú Sáu's cap, helmets, the elder's cane, the shopper's basket, phones
    (office worker, student).
  - Talking mouths: `talk`, `sell`, `phone` animate the `jaw` bone.
* **Dog Vện** (`dog.glb`, put it in group `dog` / meta `npc_kind = "dog"` for the chase music):
  `idle walk(1.1 m/s) run(3.6 m/s) bark(one-shot) sleep sniff growl`.
* **Birds** (`pigeon`, `crow` = Lão Quạ, `sparrow`): `idle peck walk fly takeoff land` (+ crow `caw`, `hop`).
  The spread flight wings are a separate part of the mesh driven by `upperarm_L/R`; ground clips scale those
  bones to 0.01 (wings hidden, the folded wings on the flanks show), `fly` scales them to 1. Always go
  `takeoff` → `fly` → `land` (they blend the scale), and do not blend a ground clip with `fly` for long.
  Flight speed suggestion: pigeon 6–9 m/s, crow 5–7 m/s, sparrow 4–6 m/s.
* **Mouse** `idle run(1.0 m/s)`, **gecko** `idle run(0.6 m/s)` (on walls rotate the node so its +Y is the wall
  normal), **koi** `swim` (+ `idle` slow hover), origin at the belly, swims toward +Z (~0.35 m/s).
* Cat-sense outline target: the `Body` MeshInstance3D of each model.
